Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Summary

This site was built so that your pictures stay on your device. This policy explains what information is and is not collected.

2. Your images

Images you add are processed locally in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. They are not uploaded, stored or seen by us or by any third party. Hidden data inside your pictures, such as location, is not copied into the files this tool creates.

3. Live statistics

To show how many images have been resized and how many people are online, your browser opens an anonymous connection to our counter service on Cloudflare. It sends only a number, the count of images you resized. We do not store your IP address, name, file names or any identifier for these statistics.

4. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused service that does not use cookies and does not track you across websites, to count page views.

5. Cookies and local storage

We save your choices, such as theme and language, in your browser's local storage, and the choice to hide the language suggestion in its session storage. This stays on your device. Advertising partners may set cookies as described in the next section.

6. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are personalized only after you give consent in the message shown by a Google-certified consent management platform. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy and cookie settings link in the footer.

7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not store personal data about you, there is usually nothing to delete, but you can write to theadvaithsonne@gmail.com with any request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact
